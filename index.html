One-time setup (2 minutes)

Websites aren't allowed to download from YouTube or Instagram themselves, so a small free helper on your PC does that part. It only runs when you press Start helper and switches itself off after an hour unused.

  1. Install Node.js (LTS) if you don't have it.
  2. Open a terminal (Windows: press Start, type cmd, Enter), paste this and press Enter:
    npx -y github:AfeefRaza/bulk-video-transcriber --install
    You can close the terminal afterwards.
  3. Back here, press Start helper. Your browser asks once whether to open it: tick "Always allow" and click Open.
  4. For Instagram: install Firefox and log in to instagram.com there once. The helper uses that login automatically. Nothing else to do.
How your Instagram account is protected
  • Read-only: it never likes, follows, comments, posts or messages.
  • Goes at a human pace: a few seconds between profile pages and 8 to 15 seconds between videos, one at a time.
  • Daily cap of 150 Instagram videos.
  • If Instagram signals "slow down" even once, the helper stops all Instagram requests for an hour.
  • Your login never leaves your PC.

Speed tip: Chrome or Edge on a computer with a decent graphics card uses WebGPU and is much faster. Other browsers fall back to the CPU, which still works but is slower. Keep this tab open while it works.
