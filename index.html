Bulk Video Transcriber

Drop in as many videos as you want and get a separate text transcript for each one. It runs in your browser, so your files never leave your computer. Free, with no account and no API key.

Drop videos here or click to choose MP4, WebM, MOV, MP3, WAV, M4A and more. Pick as many as you like.
The model downloads once on first use, then it's cached by your browser.

Speed tip: Chrome or Edge on a computer with a decent graphics card uses WebGPU and is much faster. Other browsers fall back to the CPU, which still works but is slower. Keep this tab open while it works.
